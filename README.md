You know that moment: the grid is silent, engines screaming, five red lights
glowing one by one... then they all go out and the race begins. In real F1,
the best drivers react in roughly 0.2 seconds. This game lets you find out
whether you'd survive on the grid or get left behind by the entire field.

Built by **Asomi** as part of my journey from F1 content creator into tech,
data and engineering. Yes, I made a racing game to learn how to code. No, I
am not sorry.

How to Play

1. **Open the game** and get ready on the grid.
2. **Watch the lights.** Five red lights come on, one after another.
3. **Wait for it...** after a random delay, they ALL go out.
4. **React!** Click (or tap) the moment the lights disappear.
5. **Check your time** and see how you stack up.

**Don't jump the start!** Click too early and you get a false start. In F1
that means a penalty. Here it means public shame.
